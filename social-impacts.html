Social Impacts of ChatGPT

Bias

The use of this technology perpetuates bias because bias is baked into ChatGPT's algorithm. It is not always intentional, it's just that data historically is biased, often towards white males. And then AI is trained on this biased data and reproduces these patterns. For example, biases may appear in job application tracking technologies and unintentionally harm minorities' chances at being hired because the company trained the system on its hiring history which could predominantly feature white males. In the context of my own use, such bias could appear in writing suggestions or just general information provided by ChatGPT. This requires me to evaluate its outputs and ensure that what I write or create with ChatGPT remains accurate and aligns with my personal views.

References

O’Neil, C. (2016). Ineligible to serve: Getting a job. In Weapons of math destruction (pp. 105–122). Crown Books.

Digital identity

AI and algorithms already shape many aspects of our daily life, from what we buy to the music and television we consume to how we communicate. ChatGPT contributes to this idea because it influences our school, work, and regular life. The work people make with ChatGPT like essays, code, etc., becomes part of who they are academically and professionally. It’s the same socially. Emails, texts, social media content, etc., that people make and share with ChatGPT becomes part of who they are.

ChatGPT can also influence how we understand ourselves. Research suggests that AI systems can begin to replace internal processes like introspection and self-reflection by offering their own interpretations of our thoughts and feelings. People may begin to trust these interpretations more than their own, which can weaken our internal processes as we rely on AI to explain experiences instead of understanding them ourselves. In my own use, this could appear in moments if I were to turn to ChatGPT for something like emotional or relationship advice instead of reflecting on my own. To reduce these effects, it is important to limit the use of ChatGPT for aspects like this, ensuring that one understands their own thoughts and emotions. Also to combat some of these effects on internal introspection, I have begun journaling.

References

Joseph, J. (2025). The algorithmic self: how AI is reshaping human identity, introspection, and agency. Frontiers in Psychology, 16. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1645795
